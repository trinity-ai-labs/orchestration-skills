# Adjacent

**Adjacent** is the verdict an outstanding item takes when two things hold at once: the arc's stated **goal
is fully met without it**, and the item is **genuinely a different arc**. Both or neither — one condition
alone is some other verdict, and the word names the pair rather than either half.

**The conditions are ORDERED and the first one gates.** Whether the item is an arc in its own right is weighed
only after the goal is established as met without it. Read the other way round, a heavy item the goal actually
needs reads as an arc of its own and leaves with this verdict on it, which is the one outcome the order exists
to make unreachable: an item that is both at once is a defect in how the arc was planned, not a verdict.

**It is the NARROW reading of *different arc*, and narrower than both *separable* and *real work worth
doing*.** Either of those as the bar makes every finding Adjacent. The word means the item carries a whole
unit of work of its own — its own worktree, its own pull request, its own gate — where an item that is merely
separable carries an edit. An item the goal does not need and that is not another arc is therefore not
Adjacent at all.

**The verdict is a fact about the ITEM against ONE arc's goal**, not about the item's merit, its size or its
urgency. The same item is Adjacent to one arc and forced into another, and neither reading contradicts the
other; size answers how work is carried and never whether it is in.

**An Adjacent item leaves the arc as a filed issue tracked as its own arc**, which is what distinguishes this
verdict from every other one: it is the single disposition whose item stops being the loop's to carry.

**It is a CYCLE's disposition and not a discharge.** An item stays Adjacent for as long as its filing is
outstanding, so *how many items are still Adjacent* and *how many cycles the oldest has been* are quantities
about a live arc rather than a record of something concluded.
